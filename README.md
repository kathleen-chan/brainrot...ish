# Brainrot!... ish

Welcome to **BRAINROT!... ish** This is a collection of fun and interactive mini-games designed to test your knowledge, speed, and reflexes!

Play different games, compete for high scores, and challenge yourself to climb the leaderboard.

## Features

- **Multiple Mini-Games:** Choose from a variety of games, each with its own unique challenge:
  - **Math:** Test your math skills.
  - **Clicker:** Challenge your clicking speed.
  - **Filler:** Play a fun interactive game.
  - **Type Racer:** Test your typing speed.
  - **Hangman:** Guess the hidden word before running out of attempts.
  - **Multi Clicker:** Take on another clicking challenge.
- **Leaderboard:** Track player scores and compare your performance with others.
- **Player Registration:** Register and manage player profiles.
- **Settings:** Customize your experience with sound and theme options.
- **Interactive Interface:** Easily navigate between games and menus.

## How to Play

1. Visit the [game website](https://kathleen-chan.github.io/hangman/).
2. Click **Play!** to get started.
3. Register a new player or choose an existing player.
4. Select a mini-game from the game menu.
5. Play, earn points, and aim for a high score!
6. Check the leaderboard to see how you rank.
